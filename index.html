From the practice notes

What the evidence says.

Every other Wednesday I read one piece of current research related to massage therapy or pain science, and share my plain-language summary. A few recent ones below. The rest are on the newsletter.

No. 014 · Headache & Tension

Scalp massage and tension-type headache: what the trials actually show.

A 2023 systematic review found moderate-quality evidence for cranial soft-tissue work reducing both frequency and intensity of episodic tension headaches over a six-week protocol.

Read summary →

No. 013 · Stress & Cortisol

Cortisol after a 60-minute session: smaller drop than expected, longer effect than expected.

A look at the meta-analyses on massage and salivary cortisol. The acute drop is real but modest; the more interesting finding is sleep quality on the night that follows.

Read summary →

No. 012 · Low Back Pain

NCCIH on massage for non-specific low back pain: where it fits in the stepped-care model.

The National Center's current guidance places massage alongside exercise and CBT as first-line conservative care. Useful framing for anyone managing a chronic flare-up.
