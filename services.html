Signature

Neck, scalp & face.

The treatment people remember and refer their friends to. Slow, deliberate work through the cervical spine, suboccipitals, scalp, jaw, and face, areas most clinical massage rushes through or skips entirely.

Available as the focus of a 60- or 75-minute session, or as the closing 20 minutes of any longer booking. No upcharge, it's part of the service.

What to Expect

Your first session.

A walk-through of the steps from booking to follow-up, so nothing about it feels uncertain.

  1. 01

    Book online.

    Pick a time in Jane, then complete your health-history and consent forms through the emailed link.

  2. 02

    I arrive.

    A few minutes early. Setup takes 10 minutes. We chat through what you're hoping for.

  3. 03

    Treatment.

    Pressure and focus adapted to you. Consent checked at every transition. Music or quiet, your call.

  4. 04

    Follow-up.

    A few home-care notes by email. Direct-billing receipt or Stripe receipt, your choice.
