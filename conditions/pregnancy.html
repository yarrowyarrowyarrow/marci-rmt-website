What I Treat

Pregnancy massage.

What it is

Pregnancy massage is ordinary therapeutic massage adapted for a changing body, addressing the low back, hips, and legs that carry most of the added load.

What it feels like

Low back and pelvic ache that builds through the day, tight hips, swollen legs, and difficulty finding a comfortable position for any length of time.

Massage considerations

How I approach it.

  • Sessions are side-lying with full bolstering, never on the abdomen and never with a cutout table, from the second trimester onward.
  • Work stays moderate through the legs, and I ask about any swelling, cramping, or clotting history before beginning.
  • Any high-risk pregnancy needs clearance from your care provider first, and I am glad to work alongside them.
What the evidence says

The research, briefly.

  • Trials report reduced back and leg pain and improved mood following massage during pregnancy.
  • Reviews of massage in pregnancy find it well tolerated with a low adverse-event profile when standard precautions are followed.
  • Reported benefits extend to sleep quality and anxiety as well as musculoskeletal pain.
  1. Field, T., et al. (2010). Pregnancy massage reduces prematurity, low birthweight and postpartum depression. Infant Behavior and Development, 33(4), 409–414.
  2. Smith, C. A., et al. (2018). Massage, reflexology and other manual methods for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (3), CD009290.

Educational, not diagnostic. If symptoms are new, severe, or changing, please see a physician first.
